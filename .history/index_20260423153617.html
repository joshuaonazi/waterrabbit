How to Buy RyuJin (RYU)
on Uniswap

1

Create a Wallet

If you don't have one yet, start by creating a MetaMask, TrustWallet, or any preferred wallet. MetaMask allows you to buy, sell, send, and receive $RYU. Ensure to keep your recovery phrase in a safe place.

2

Get Some Ethereum (ETH)

If your wallet doesn't have ETH, you can purchase it directly through MetaMask, transfer it from another wallet, or buy it on a centralized exchange and then withdraw it to your wallet.

3

Go to Uniswap

After you have Ethereum in your MetaMask wallet, visit app.uniswap.org, click 'Connect to a wallet,' and select MetaMask to connect your wallet to Uniswap.

4

Swap ETH for $RYU

Choose the amount you wish to trade and set your slippage to 0.5–1%. Click Swap and confirm the transaction. When your wallet prompts you, confirm the action.
