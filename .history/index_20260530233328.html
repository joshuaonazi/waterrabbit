About Water Rabbit

Water Rabbit ($WAR) is a deflationary token on Binance Smart Chain, launched in the year 2022 by Shanvere DAO. Its name draws from the Chinese Lunar Calendar (The Year of The Water Rabbit) and the Zodiac Jade Rabbit's symbolism of patience, endurance, and transformation is woven into the project's
long-term philosophy.

From its first day, the project positioned itself against the
meme-coin stereotype. The core mission is to evolve $WAR into a functioning digital currency, one that operates as a medium of exchange, a store of value, and a unit of account. These are the three classical functions of money. Three years after launching at a $755 market cap, the project is still here, still building, and still on track.

Frequently Asked Questions about $WAR

Water Rabbit is a deflationary digital currency built on the Binance Smart Chain (BSC).
Originally launched on July 27, 2022, by Shanvere DAO , the project's long-term mission is to evolve $WAR from a meme token
into a fully functioning digital currency that operates as a medium of exchange, a store of value, and a unit of account.

The token was launched by Daddy Zeejay DAO / Shanvere DAO via a Fair Launch.
This means there was no presale and no private allocations given to insiders or early buyers.

The project's architecture is designed to make predatory developer behavior impossible through two main mechanisms:

  • Ownership Renounced: The deployer wallet has permanently surrendered control of the contract.
    Nobody, including the founding team can change the code, alter the tax rates, or mint new tokens.
  • Liquidity Locked: 99% of the liquidity pool is permanently locked on UNCX, a reputable Binance Smart Chain liquidity locker.

$WAR implements a 9% total transaction tax on every buy and sell:

  • 3% Burn Fee: Automatically and permanently destroys tokens by sending them to a dead wallet.
    This fee is hard-coded and cannot be turned off.
  • 6% Operations Fee: Directed toward marketing, community initiatives, charity and ongoing ecosystem development.

Unlike typical low-market-cap tokens, Water Rabbit features three live ecosystem utilities built to solve common crypto market failures:

  • WARGUARD: A multi-chain token scanner and security intelligence dashboard.
    It helps users evaluate a contract's safety, liquidity metrics,
    and holder concentrations in plain language before risking capital.
  • WAR LIBRARY: A structured, beginner-friendly educational series containing over 12 modules that
    explain foundational blockchain concepts like cryptography, nodes, proof of work, and adoption mechanics.
  • WARCAST: The official audio layer of the project available on Spotify. It converts modules from the WAR Library into accessible,
    bite-sized audio episodes (11–19 minutes long) to help onboard newcomers without the usual market "hype".

  • Decentralized Exchange (DEX): PancakeSwap V2 (WAR/BNB Pair)
  • Centralized Exchange (CEX):P2PB2B (WAR/USDT Pair)
  • Tracking & Charts: WARGUARD, CoinMarketCap, CoinGecko, DexScreener, Bogged Finance, and Poocoin
