About
Water Rabbit

Water Rabbit ($WAR) is a deflationary token on Binance Smart Chain, launched in the year 2022 by Shanvere DAO. Its name draws from the Chinese Lunar Calendar (The Year of The Water Rabbit) and the Zodiac Jade Rabbit's symbolism of patience, endurance, and transformation is woven into the project's long-term philosophy.

From its first day, the project positioned itself against the meme-coin stereotype. The core mission is to evolve $WAR into a functioning digital currency — one that operates as a medium of exchange, a store of value, and a unit of account. These are the three classical functions of money. Three years after launching at a $755 market cap, the project is still here, still building, and still on track.

Security Architecture

  • Ownership Renounced: The deployer wallet permanently surrendered contract control.
  • Liquidity Locked: 99% of the liquidity pool is locked on UNCX.
  • Contract Audited: Independent security review completed with no major issues identified.
  • Verified on BscScan: Full source code is publicly visible. No hidden functions, no backdoors.
